# Source trust policy

Sources are registered before ingestion. Tier 1 is an official Nation, government, school, funder, or verified organization source; Tier 2 is a reputable partner or direct employer; Tier 3 is community-submitted or editorial material awaiting review. No tier converts an unverified claim into fact.

Show publisher, retrieval date, original link, applicability caveats, and freshness to users. Automated extraction may propose fields but cannot silently alter authoritative records. Honor terms, robots controls, licensing, correction requests, and takedowns.
