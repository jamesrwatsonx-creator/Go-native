# Privacy model

Privacy is contextual control: users choose what enters Go Native, which workspace can access it, and when it leaves.

- Separate personal and organizational contexts with explicit workspace grants.
- Request data at the moment it becomes useful; support partial profiles and coarse location.
- Encrypt sensitive documents at rest; issue short-lived access links; record access events.
- Gmail/email access is opt-in, purpose-limited, revocable, and never required for matching.
- Default analytics to aggregate/de-identified reporting; prohibit sale of personal data.
- Support export, correction, deletion, consent history, and retention policies subject to lawful obligations.

Community governance, data residency, and Indigenous data sovereignty requirements are deployment decisions, not afterthoughts. Obtain legal and community review before launch.
